Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prime Intelligence CRM collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website, forms, CRM systems, automations, calendars, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, website, social media links, appointment details, form responses, CRM activity, and messages you submit through our systems.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide CRM, automation, onboarding, and marketing services.
  • To respond to inquiries and schedule appointments.
  • To send service-related messages, reminders, follow-ups, and support communications.
  • To improve our website, offers, systems, and client experience.

3. SMS and Email Communications

If you provide your phone number or email address, we may contact you about your inquiry, account, appointment, or services. Marketing messages are only sent where appropriate consent exists. You may opt out of SMS by replying STOP and may unsubscribe from email when an unsubscribe option is provided.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers needed to operate our CRM, communications, hosting, payment, scheduling, analytics, and automation systems.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website or connected tools may use cookies, pixels, analytics, or tracking technologies to understand visitor behavior, improve performance, and support advertising or retargeting.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information where required by applicable law. You may also opt out of promotional communications.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 13.

9. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
