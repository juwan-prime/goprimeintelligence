Website FAQ

Know what you're buying.

What exactly is a Revenue Website?

It's a streamlined one-page business website with lead capture, a simple CRM, lead notifications, hosting, domain connection and foundational SEO/AEO. It intentionally does not include automated follow-up or AI.

Can I add automation later?

Yes. The Revenue Website is the entry point. If your business later needs automated follow-up, missed-call text back, more advanced workflows or AI, those systems can be added through an appropriate Prime Intelligence package.

Do I own my domain?

Your domain remains your business asset when it is owned by you. Prime Intelligence can connect the domain to the website as part of the applicable setup.

Does SEO guarantee Google rankings?

No. Foundational SEO and AEO are included where stated, but no specific search-engine or AI-search ranking is guaranteed.

What happens after the custom website is paid?

After the applicable build/payment term, Website Care is $99/month for managed hosting, SSL/security, platform maintenance, basic technical support and monitoring.

What if I need something outside the listed scope?

New pages, major redesigns, advanced functionality, custom development, specialty integrations and other substantial additions can be quoted separately before the work begins.
