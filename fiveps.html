The Principle Behind the Playbook

Preparation changes performance.

The Five P's is built around a simple idea: you do not need to be a guru to build a better real estate business. You need preparation, consistency, professional presentation, and systems that actually work.

More than a book.

The Five P's is becoming a connected learning and implementation ecosystem: a published playbook, a free community course in development, formal continuing education, real-world media examples, and practical systems that help turn the framework into action.

The Framework

Five areas. One stronger business.

The course and playbook move from how you present yourself and your listings to how you stay visible, organize opportunity, follow up, and use technology without losing the human relationship.

01

Your Brand

Personal and professional branding, target audience, reputation, online presence, consistency, and how the Realtor is presented beyond an individual listing.

02

Property Media

Photography, video, drone, 360 tours, property preparation, media rights and intentional visual presentation.

03

Marketing

Property marketing, personal marketing, content pillars, repurposing, consistency and visibility beyond active listings.

04

Systems

CRM, lead pipelines, follow-up, scheduling, automation, AI and technology that supports rather than replaces the human element.

05

Action Plan

Review the framework, identify priorities, build an implementation plan and turn ideas into practical next steps.

Five P's FAQ

What is The Five P's Realtor Playbook?

It is a practical framework created by Juwan Davis for real estate professionals, connecting branding, property media, marketing, systems, automation, AI and implementation planning around the principle “Proper Preparation Prevents Poor Performance.”

Is the book available now?

Yes. The Five P's Realtor Playbook is published in Kindle, paperback and hardcover editions.

Is the free Five P's course available now?

Not yet. The free community course is currently in development and is planned to include nearly three hours of training plus resources and ongoing education.

Is the course approved for CE credit?

West Virginia CE approval is currently pending. The Virginia CE application has not yet been submitted; submission is planned. The dedicated CE page will carry the current status as approvals and submissions change.

Is this only about technology and AI?

No. Technology is one part of the framework. The Five P's begins with branding, professional presentation, property media and marketing, then connects those areas to systems, follow-up, automation and practical AI.

Where do I go for CRM, automation or AI implementation?

Prime Intelligence is the implementation side for CRM, automation, AI voice, websites, reputation, follow-up and business systems.
