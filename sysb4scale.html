Five practical systems that can help your business capture opportunities, stay organized, communicate better, and build a stronger foundation.

The Five Systems

Get the foundation right.
Then scale.

Scaling a broken process doesn't fix it. It usually creates a bigger version of the same problem. Start with the systems your business needs to handle opportunities consistently.

SYSTEM 01

Lead Capture

Attention doesn't automatically become opportunity. Your business needs a clear path for someone to raise their hand, request information, schedule, call, message, or tell you what they need.

Don't generate more traffic until you know what you're doing with the traffic you already have.
SYSTEM 02

Follow-Up

Not every customer is ready today. A follow-up system helps make sure interested people aren't forgotten simply because you're busy or they weren't ready during the first conversation.

Don't lose tomorrow's customer because you forgot to follow up today.
SYSTEM 03

Your CRM

Leads shouldn't live across text messages, email, spreadsheets, sticky notes, and your memory. A CRM gives your business one place to organize contacts, conversations, opportunities, and activity.

You don't need the most complicated CRM. You need one your business will actually use.
SYSTEM 04

Automatic Scheduling

Customers shouldn't have to play phone tag just to get on your calendar. A good scheduling system makes it easier to choose a time, receive confirmations, get reminders, and stay connected.

Make it easy for people to do business with you.
SYSTEM 05

Reputation

Happy customers are one of your strongest marketing assets, but most businesses leave reviews to chance. A reputation system helps make requesting feedback consistent and gives future customers more proof when they're researching your business.

Build your reputation one satisfied customer at a time.
