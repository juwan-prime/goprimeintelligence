Before Your Recording Session

A little preparation makes the setup much smoother.

Your avatar footage itself can be captured quickly, but the full session includes setup, lighting, environment changes and voice capture. Your selected package determines how much recording and training time to plan for.

  • Have wardrobe choices ready before the session
  • Choose quiet, clean recording areas when recording on location
  • Avoid tight patterns or distracting clothing when possible
  • Be prepared to read the provided voice-training material
  • Have access to the email/account that will own your AI tools
Important

Authorized avatars only.

Prime Intelligence only assists with authorized digital twins of the person participating in the recording. Identity verification, consent steps and any platform-required authorization must be completed by the person being replicated.
